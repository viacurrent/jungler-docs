# Welcome to Jungler

Jungler watches social media for the conversations and people that matter to you — and hands you the data in a form your team can actually use.

You get three tools, each tuned for a different question.

## Profile engagement monitoring

Pick the profiles and companies you care about. Jungler tells you who's commenting on their posts and who's reacting — so you can see which of their buyers, partners, or employees are paying attention.

## Keyword monitoring

Write a search ("looking for" + "CRM", for example) and Jungler keeps watching for new posts that match. An AI layer filters each post against your own definition of relevant, so you don't drown in noise.

## Post engagement (Workbooks)

Paste a specific post URL and Jungler extracts every commenter and reactor, deduplicated, with enriched contact data. Use it when you've spotted a post worth harvesting — a competitor's launch, an industry thread, a viral complaint.

---

All three feed the same place: your signals dashboard, the [API](./api/index.md), and any integration you've connected (Clay, Slack, Google Sheets, HeyReach, Expandi, or your own webhook).
