# How Jungler Works

Jungler helps you discover and engage with relevant social media conversations through a simple three-step process.

## The Process

### 1. Search Setup

Define your search criteria using keywords, Boolean operators, and filters. Jungler continuously monitors posts matching your search parameters.

**Example search:**
```
"recommendations" AND CRM
```

### 2. AI Filtering

Every post is automatically analyzed by AI according to your custom prompt. Posts are classified as "Relevant" or "Not Relevant" based on your specific criteria.

**Custom filter example:**
> "Only show posts from people looking for marketing automation tools"

Or use the default buying-intent filter by leaving the prompt blank.

### 3. Continuous Updates

Searches are automatically rerun every 24 hours, ensuring your feed stays current with the latest matching conversations.

---

## What Gets Analyzed?

| Data Point | What It Examines |
|------------|------------------|
| **Post content** | Full text and context of the post |
| **Author information** | Job title, company, and profile details |
| **Engagement signals** | Comments, reactions, and conversation patterns |
